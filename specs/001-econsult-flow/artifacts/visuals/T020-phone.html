Reported, not fixed. Measurements are in points; screenshots are 3 px per point.

Blocking · scenario 6's own criterion

The “Force offline” switch is clipped by the right screen edge at AX3XL

The row's box ends at 386 pt, the screen at 402 pt, and the native control — 62.7 pt wide, measured at the default size — starts at 346.7 pt. Its right cap therefore lands near 409 pt: about 7 pt of the switch is off screen, and the track colour runs to the final pixel column with no rounded end.

Mechanism (observed, not fixed). styles.row in src/app/dev-settings.tsx is flexDirection: "row" with justifyContent: "space-between" and a plain <Text style={text.body}>Force offline</Text>. React Native defaults flexShrink to 0, so at AX3XL the label keeps its full single-line intrinsic width (glyphs run from 20 pt to about 330 pt), the 16 pt gap follows, and the switch is pushed past the row edge. The label neither shrinks nor wraps.

Impact is visual, not functional. The row is 86 pt tall and still toggles from the label side — pressing (100, 560) flipped its value 0 → 1 and back — and enough of the pill shows to read its state. Apply was proven reachable by use: pressed at (201, 722) at AX3XL, it applied and returned home.

Minor

The switch is not vertically centred on its label

styles.row sets alignItems: "center", but the painted pill sits at the top of the row at both text sizes. The offset equals half the row's spare height, so the control behaves as if top-aligned or stretched rather than centred. The native switch is hidden from the accessibility tree, so only its painted band is measurable and the mechanism was not determined.

Minor · outside the declared scenarios

The modal has no way out other than “Apply and go home”

dev-settings is registered with presentation: "modal" and no header close button, so a developer who opens the screen to look and changes nothing must either apply — which the screen's own copy says clears cached practice data — or swipe the sheet away. Noted for the flag gate, not raised against this step.

  • Names and roles. All fifteen radios expose their label plus value="radio button, checked|unchecked", which is how iOS surfaces accessibilityRole="radio" together with accessibilityState.checked. The toggle row is one Switch node named “Force offline”; the native switch inside it is correctly absent from the tree, so there is no duplicate control.
  • Touch targets. Every single-line radio measures exactly 48.00 pt; the two-line prc-0421 row 65.33. The switch row and Apply are 48.00.
  • The whole row is the switch target. Pressing (100, 720) — the label side, 200 pt from the pill — moved the value 0 → 1.
  • Applied settings persist. Re-entering the screen showed prc-0873 checked after scenario 2 and Force offline at 1 after scenario 4: the draft seeds from applied state, not from the env defaults.

Scenario 6 · the largest text size, end to end

6 596 pt of content in a 742 pt viewport, so eleven frames cover the page. Everything above the switch row scales and wraps correctly: practice options reach 448.67 pt over five lines, option rows 106 pt, and Apply wraps to two lines at 187.67 pt. No two nodes overlap at any scroll position.

What this device cannot show

  • accessibilityRole="alert" on the offline banner. iOS exposes the grouped banner as Other with the folded label; no alert role or live-region trait appears in any snapshot mode — the same limit T019 hit. Presence, position and copy are observed; the role is source-only.
  • accessibilityHint on Send. The driver carries no hint field, so the wording “You're offline. Sending needs a connection.” (OFFLINE_HINT) could not be read off the device. What is observed is the consequence it describes: Send reports [disabled] and does not respond to a press.
  • accessibilityRole="header" on the screen title is likewise not exposed as a trait; the node appears as StaticText with the right label.

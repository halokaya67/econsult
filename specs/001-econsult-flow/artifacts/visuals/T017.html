Flags and notes

  • Fragile The fix passes with almost no margin at AX3XL. The error box overhangs the viewport by 9.33 pt and only about 2 px of inked clearance remains below the second line. One more wrapped line in the question label, a larger lineHeight.heading, a longer required-question string, or a shorter viewport than this 758 pt one would push the error’s second line back off screen. Not a defect in the current build; worth knowing before the copy or the type scale changes.
  • Out of scope The step heading “A few questions from your practice” still has no upper bound on Dynamic Type scaling — 814.33 pt tall against a 758 pt viewport, more than a full screen of heading. tokens.ts caps font scale for the step chip and the header buttons but not for screen headings. Unchanged from the earlier passes.
  • Unverified VoiceOver announcement and screen-reader focus order are still untested — neither could be captured on this device. A VoiceOver pass is needed before the accessibility claim is fully closed.

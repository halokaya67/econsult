1c — the error is 522 pt below the fold

The red message is rendered after the three radio options. At AX3XL the question block is about 1436 pt tall against a 758 pt viewport, so the label and its error can never be on screen together — scrolling the label to the top is the one choice that guarantees the error is off it.

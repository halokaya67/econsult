What this walkthrough found

The step renders and behaves correctly at the default text size: the required-answer check holds the user on the screen, the error reads clearly, and the keyboard never traps the primary action. Two parts of the contract do not hold. At the largest accessibility text size the error appears above the viewport and the view does not scroll to it, so the tap looks like it did nothing. And in the accessibility tree the error text is attached to no field — the choice question has no group element at all, so nothing carries it into an accessible name.

Defects

Reported, not fixed. Every claim below comes from the accessibility tree or the screenshot above it.

Blocking

No scroll to the required-answer error at large text sizes

At accessibility-extra-extra-extra-large, tapping Continue with the required choice unanswered renders the error roughly 140 pt above the viewport and the scroll view does not move. The screen is visually identical before and after the tap, so the step appears to do nothing. At the default text size the error happens to fall on screen, which is why this only surfaces under Dynamic Type.

Blocking

Error text is not folded into the field’s accessible name

After the error, the three options still read Less than a week / 1 to 4 weeks / Longer than a month with value radio button, unchecked, and the question label is unchanged. The error is a standalone text node beside them. There is also no group element wrapping the choice question — the three radios are siblings of the question label — so no element exists whose accessible name could carry the error.

Minor

Step heading has no upper bound on Dynamic Type scaling

“A few questions from your practice” grows to 814 pt at AX3XL, more than one full 758 pt viewport, and breaks mid-word. A reader at that size scrolls past a screen and a half of heading before reaching the first question.

Not verified

Announcement and screen-reader focus could not be observed

The contract also calls for the error to be announced once and for the field to take screen-reader focus. VoiceOver was not running and the driver exposes no focus or announcement channel, so neither claim was confirmed or refuted here. They need a VoiceOver pass.

Behaviour confirmed working

  • No navigation on the invalid submit, at either text size.
  • The error clears as soon as an option is chosen.
  • Answers survive a back-navigation from the Message step.
  • Radios and Continue are 48 pt at the default size and grow from there.
  • Nothing overlaps or clips at AX3XL — every element’s box ends before the next one starts.

Defects
Header “Home” label is clipped at large accessibility text sizes defect
recipient-selected-largest.png · recipient-selected-largest-scrolled.png

At content size accessibility-extra-extra-extra-large the Home button’s text scales with the system setting but the navigation bar keeps its fixed height, so only the top half of the word renders. The control still works and still reads correctly to VoiceOver, but sighted users at large text sizes cannot read the one back control this step added. Its box also grows to 178 pt wide, leaving a 16 pt gap to the “Recipient” title.

Continue keeps its “choose a recipient first” hint after a card is selected observation
src/app/econsult/recipient.tsx:90 — source-read, not observable on the device

The accessibilityHint is passed unconditionally, so once a recipient is chosen and the button is enabled VoiceOver still announces “Choose who you are writing to first” after the label. The hint is correct while the button is disabled; it should drop away with the disabled state. Accessibility hints are not exposed by the simulator, so this was read from source.

Loading state withholds the step chip and heading observation
recipient-loading.png

While the care team loads, the skeletons replace the whole body including “Step 1 of 3” and “Who are you writing to?”, so the screen carries no context beyond its title and the content jumps down when data arrives. Keeping the chip and heading mounted would remove the shift.
